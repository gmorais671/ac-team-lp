# Imagens dos cards Como funciona

Geradas com a ferramenta integrada imagegen. Versões WebP otimizadas em `public/images/steps/`.
São fotografias geradas, com fade e aproximação via CSS; não são vídeos.

## avaliacao.webp

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Create a portrait photograph for a fitness coaching website card, full bleed 3:4 composition. Real physical assessment equipment on a charcoal gym bench: premium black body weight scale, coiled white measuring tape with realistic markings, and a close crop of a trainer's hands measuring an adult athlete's upper arm circumference. No faces. Anatomically correct hands. Dramatic realistic studio photography, soft red side light, black and charcoal setting, fine material textures. Main subjects centered with generous margins for portrait crop. No overlays, no logos, no watermark.

## planejamento.webp

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Portrait 3:4 full bleed editorial photograph for a personal training website card. Close view of a real trainer's hand writing checkmarks with a black pen on a white paper workout and nutrition protocol clipped to a dark clipboard on a charcoal desk. Rows of neatly handwritten notes and checkboxes, no large readable headline required. Small blurred meal container and dumbbell at edge of frame. Real paper and metal texture, cinematic soft lighting with subtle red accent from the side, deep charcoal background. Main clipboard centered for portrait crop. No logos, overlays, watermark.

## acompanhamento.webp

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Portrait 3:4 full bleed editorial photograph for a personal training website card. Close view of a real paper desk calendar on a charcoal desk, a hand holding a red pen marking completed days with red ticks, several prior days already checked, evoking consistent fitness progress over weeks. Realistic calendar grid and paper texture, black metal spiral binding, cinematic soft light with subtle red side lighting, dark gym coaching office blurred behind. Main calendar centered with generous margins for portrait crop. No logos, overlays, watermark.
